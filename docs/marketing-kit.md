## 1. The messaging house

**Roof (the promise):** *Your phone is a supercomputer.*
**Pillar 1 — It knows your machine.** Vertically integrated with the OS and silicon; what fits runs on-device — instant, private, $0.
**Pillar 2 — It bursts to YOUR cloud.** The right-sized machine in the customer's own Google Cloud — newest GPU/TPU, priced before it runs, deleted after.
**Pillar 3 — It's open.** A2A + AP2 + MCP: other agents can discover, price, and use it.
**Foundation (the trust line):** *Your cloud. Your keys. Your receipt.*

One-liner by audience:
- **Consumer:** "Ask for something your phone could never do. Adam does it."
- **Practitioner:** "Stop shrinking the model. Stop queueing. $118, 92 minutes, the full 70B."
- **Enterprise:** "Every employee already carries a supercomputer. Turn it on."
- **Ecosystem:** "The supercomputing node of the One network — discoverable by any agent."

## 2. The differentiation story (the marketing cut of the side-by-side)

Category framing — **we are not**: a GPU reseller, a cloud, a notebook platform, a cluster. **We are** the *placement intelligence* — the agent that decides where compute should run and makes it one seamless act. Campaign expression:

> **"Everyone sells you a place to run compute. Adam decides where compute should run — and the place is one you already own."**

Contrast pairs for creative (each is one ad):
- *The queue vs the answer.* (cluster wait vs receipt)
- *The idle box vs the vanishing machine.* (24/7 burn vs pay-per-second, torn down)
- *The shrunk model vs the real one.* (+18 points)
- *The guess vs the price.* (console roulette vs cost-before-run)
- *Their account vs your keys.* (managed clouds vs BYOC)

## 3. Value-creation proof (usable numbers, always with the honesty bar)

$118 / +18 pts full 70B · $2 / 5 TB backtest · ~93% vs standing box · $0 on-device (2 of 6) · phone-to-TPU protein folding. *Composites with transparent figures; label sims as sims.*
